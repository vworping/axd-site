[pet.]

personal engagement tool

I wanted a way to show people what I’m working on while we’re talking. PET puts my projects on a small touch display and lets someone say hello from their phone.

The interaction

  1. 01

    Start on PET.

    Tap hello. Scan the QR code with your phone.

  2. 02

    Say it back.

    Share your name and what we’re talking about. Your hello appears on the badge.

  3. 03

    Carry it forward.

    An NFC card opens my profile with the topic you agreed to share—a reminder of how we met.

The OS

Preview controls · simulated radio and phone events

Making it

This is my first time working with ESP. I’m using AI coding help to figure out the firmware, HTML/CSS to try out the screens, and CAD for the enclosure. Along the way, I’ve been wiring the NFC reader, testing card links on my phone, and revising the case.

Built around a Waveshare touch display ↗, a NULLLAB NFC reader ↗, and a 3.7 V LiPo battery ↗.
